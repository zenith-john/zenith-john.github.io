  • 作为文学爱好者：我喜欢寓言式的精炼的富有想象力的作品。列举几个我喜欢的作家：屈原、鲁迅、卡夫卡、卡尔维诺、马尔克斯、帕慕克。目前我对唐宋时期的诗词最感兴趣。参考【诗心自用】。

  • 作为语言爱好者：我学习过多门语言，虽然基本上只掌握了一些皮毛，包括：中文（包括文言文、普通话和吴语«宁波方言»，母语）、英文（流利）、法语（能读 L’Étranger）、日语（一些简单句）、梵语（基本语法，进一步学习和练习中）、藏语（书面语的基本语法，进一步学习和练习中），还稍稍涉猎过一些拉丁语、阿拉伯语（MSA）和德语。目前我主要关心汉藏语言学以及翻译相关的问题。

  • 作为哲学爱好者：在众多哲学家之中，我喜欢庄子、康德、马克思、尼采、福柯。目前我的兴趣主要集中在宋明理学和古印度哲学，以及 19 世纪法国的政治思想上。

  • 作为谜题爱好者：我通关过多次 Puzzle Hunt 中，包括 CCBC12，P&KU3，CCBC15 等，具体见 Puzzle Hunt 参赛历史和计划。我编写了一些相关工具，发布在 Potior 上。

  • 作为编程爱好者：我是一个业余程序员，编程语言爱好者，Emacs 使用者。我的 Emacs 配置可以参考文章 Emacs，Lox in Rust 是我用 Rust 写的一个解释器。在 Github 上还有一些我写的其他零散的脚本和程序。参考【井蛙语海】。

  • As a literature hobbyist: I prefer allegorical, stylish and imaginative work. To name some my favourite writers: Qu Yuan, Lu Xun, Kafka, Calvino, Márquez, Pamuk. Currently, I’m interested in Shi(poetry) and Ci in Tang and Song dynasty.

  • As a language hobbyist: I have learned several languages, though mostly at a quite elementary level, including: Chinese(Mandarin and Wu dialect(Ningbo accent), native, also classical Chinese), English(fluent), French(can read L’Étranger), Japanese(Some simple sentence), Sanskrit(Basic Grammar, still learning and practicing), classical literary Tibetan(Basic Grammar, still learning and practicing). I have also known a little bit about Latin, Arabic(MSA) and German. Currently I’m interested in the study of Sino-Tibetan languages and translation.

  • As a philosophy hobbyist: Among famous philosophers, I like Zhuangzi, Kant, Marx, Nietzsche, and Foucault. I’m interested in neo-Confucianism, ancient Indian philosophy and political thought in 19th century France.

  • As a puzzle hobbyist: I have completed(?) several Puzzle Hunts, for example CCBC12, P&KU3 and CCBC15, see Puzzle Hunt history and plan for details。Several puzzle hunt related tools are published on Potior.

  • As a programming hobbyist: I am an amateur programmer, programming language hobbyist and heavy emacs user. My emacs configuration is described in Emacs. In Lox in Rust, I implement a Lox interpreter in Rust. And there are several other simple scripts and programs on Github.
